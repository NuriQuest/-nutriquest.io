How it works

Nutrition tracking,
reimagined as a game

Every meal is a quest. Every good choice earns XP. Your hero evolves with your habits.

01
📸
Scan your meal
Photo scan with AI or barcode scan for packaged food. Adjust your portion size before logging.
02
⚡
Earn XP & gems
Every scan rewards you. Combo multipliers stack when you log consistently throughout the day.
03
📋
Complete quests
4 daily quests and 2 weekly challenges refresh constantly. Hit your goals, claim your rewards.
04
⚔️
Level your hero
Your character visually evolves as you level up — stronger, more detailed, better gear. Earn it, don't buy it.
Features

Everything to make
healthy eating stick

Built around the psychology of habit formation, not willpower.

🦸
Evolving hero character
Your avatar gets visibly stronger as you hit nutrition milestones. Muscle definition, armour upgrades, elemental effects — earned through consistency, not purchases.
Core mechanic
🔥
Streaks & hearts
Build a daily logging streak. Miss a day and lose a heart. Craft streak shields from Iron Rations earned by consistent logging — or grab a freeze from the shop.
🏆
Weekly leagues
Compete in Bronze through Diamond leagues on weekly XP. Top performers get promoted. Bottom get relegated. Real stakes, real motivation.
🤖
AI meal coach
Chat with an AI coach that knows what you've eaten today. Ask anything — macro breakdowns, meal suggestions, quest strategy. Context-aware answers, not generic advice.
Pro feature
👹
Community boss raids
Every week a community boss spawns. Hit your nutrition goals to deal damage. Defeat the boss together and everyone gets gems. Miss it and nobody wins.
💎
Gem economy
Earn gems through quests and streaks. Spend them on streak protection, hero cosmetics, XP boosts, and quest perks. A real in-game economy that rewards consistency.
